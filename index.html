Skincare Consumer & Market Research

Question: What factors influence skincare preferences and purchasing decisions among Indian Gen Z consumers?

This project combines secondary research with a primary survey of 27 eligible respondents aged 18–27, then turns the findings into consumer insights, personas, strategy and a hypothetical campaign.
