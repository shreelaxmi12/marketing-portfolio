FEATURED CAMPAIGN

Know Your Skin.
Know Your Routine.

A hypothetical skincare campaign built from my consumer research — turning one clear insight into a simple marketing idea.

Consumer signal: 63% of respondents said too many products/choices was a challenge.

Core thought: Consumers don't necessarily need more skincare — they need more confidence in choosing it.
